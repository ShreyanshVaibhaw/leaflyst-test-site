Our story

Northwind Roasters started in 2018 in a small garage with one secondhand roaster and a stubborn belief that great coffee should be honest and traceable. Today we work with a handful of farms across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, paying prices well above the commodity market and returning to the same growers season after season. Our team is small on purpose. Roasting in tiny batches lets us taste and adjust constantly, chasing the sweetness and clarity that mass production tends to flatten. We believe a cup of coffee carries the work of dozens of hands, and we try to honor every one of them in how we buy, roast, and serve.
