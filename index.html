Fresh coffee, roasted in small batches

Northwind Roasters is a family-run roastery in the heart of Seattle. We source green coffee directly from growers we know by name, then roast it in small batches so every bag leaves our door at its peak. Whether you love a bright, fruit-forward single origin or a deep, chocolatey espresso blend, we have something for your morning cup. New batches are roasted every Tuesday and Friday and shipped the same day, so the coffee that reaches you is never more than a few days off the roaster. Browse our subscriptions, learn about our story, or stop by the roastery to say hello and taste what is fresh this week.
