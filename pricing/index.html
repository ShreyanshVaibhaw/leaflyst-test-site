Pricing

Our pricing is simple and flexible. A single twelve-ounce bag of single-origin coffee is sixteen dollars, and our signature blends are fifteen. Prefer to never run out? A weekly or biweekly subscription saves you ten percent on every bag and ships free anywhere in the United States. You choose the roast, the grind, and the cadence, and you can pause or cancel any time from your account. Larger households and offices can order our two-pound and five-pound bags at a reduced per-ounce rate. Every order, large or small, is roasted to order in the days before it ships, so you are always paying for coffee at its freshest.
